Design notes

  • Flowers and temple are cut from the owner's mockup (D:\own\photo_2026-11-02_12-44-37.jpg), with the baked-in text painted out and upscaled 2× (docs/design/assets). They are slightly soft — swap in high-resolution originals when available.
  • Frames, corner flourishes, the kbach flourish, diamonds and ribbons are drawn in SVG/CSS and follow the theme color. Only gold-family themes are offered because the flower art is gold.
  • Fonts: Moul (Khmer titles), Noto Serif Khmer (Khmer text), Cormorant Garamond (English).
  • Programme days and the RSVP form sit side by side from 420px wide and stack on narrow phones.
